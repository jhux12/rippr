Current mode

The site currently operates with sandbox payments, test balances and test item records. No real-money service or shipping program is launched by these draft disclosures. Current pack prices reported by the owner are $1, $5 and $15; the saved price shown at checkout/opening is the operative test price, not a permanent promise in this document.

One card per pack

Each pack awards one card. Selecting several packs buys that many separate one-card outcomes, not a physical retail booster containing multiple cards. rippr-branded packs are site presentations; branding does not imply they are manufacturer-sealed Pokémon products.

Odds and rarity

Review the selected pack’s current odds table and item pool. Rarity tiers are rippr’s site classifications and can differ from a card publisher’s printed rarity. Tier percentages aggregate item probabilities; value ranges show the minimum and maximum saved item values in that tier. Grail previews highlight possible items, not guaranteed results or a separate entitlement.

Randomness and duplicates

Outcomes are selected by the server from configured probabilities. The same card can appear more than once, and previous results do not guarantee a later rare pull. Animation length, glow colors, ripping speed and skipping do not improve odds or alter an awarded card. Opening multiple packs does not guarantee a profit, complete set or particular rarity.

Values and sellbacks

Displayed values are site reference values, which may be imported or edited and may become outdated. They are not guaranteed third-party market prices or investment returns. Review the saved amount offered before a sellback; confirmation grants site credit only. There is no cash withdrawal feature. Expected value is an average under configured probabilities, not a promise about one opening or a small batch.

Near Mint and graded cards

Ungraded cards are described as Near Mint. The intended condition standard allows minor edge or corner wear and light surface imperfections, but excludes major creases, tears, water damage or significant structural damage. Near Mint does not mean flawless, Gem Mint or guaranteed to receive a particular grade. Graded listings show the grading company and grade; inspect the actual image and details. Images, labels, card identity, grade and condition need to agree. Do not infer that a raw card is PSA graded because an unrelated promotional image shows a slab.

Keep, sell or request shipping

An awarded card is saved to inventory before the reveal completes. Keeping it retains that record; selling marks it sold and grants the shown credit. Interrupting or skipping the animation should not require paying again. Shipping remains a test request, not actual dispatch. Report missing or incorrect records rather than repeatedly purchasing.

Affiliation and trust signals

rippr is an independent service and is not presented as affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Pokémon, Nintendo, Creatures, Game Freak, PSA, TCGplayer or other displayed brands. Third-party marks identify their respective owners or products. Do not treat unverified review placeholders, generated packing illustrations or a successful sandbox payment as evidence of customer reviews, shipped orders or regulatory approval.

Eligibility, policies and support

Approved launch policy: adults 18 or older, US-only purchases restricted to cleared states, no expiration of credit, and no routine cash withdrawals. Unused purchased-credit and unopened purchased-pack requests have a planned 14-day refund/cancellation window, subject to mandatory rights and the refund policy. These launch controls are not yet active. State clearance, verification, production credit rules and fulfillment implementation remain required before live service. Ask hello@rippr.gg or Contact support if price, odds, condition, grade or credit treatment is unclear before opening.

Questions?

Contact support or email hello@rippr.gg.

Spark Points

Earn 1 Spark Point per $1 of purchased credit actually spent on pack openings; fractions are kept. Deposits alone, promotional credit, sellback credit, legacy test credit and reward packs do not earn points. Redeem 25 points for one Spark Spin using the published prize odds. Points do not expire, cannot be transferred and have no cash or withdrawal value. Daily claims and streak payouts have ended. Refunds or disputes may require review and removal of related points; a points shortfall is repaid by future earnings before more points become available. Closing an account relinquishes remaining points with your explicit confirmation. All current balances, points and prizes are sandbox-only.

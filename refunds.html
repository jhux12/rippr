Refunds & cancellations

Clear distinctions between payments, packs and sellbacks.

Current testing status

This is a review draft. Current Checkout payments, balances and items are sandbox-only. Test payments do not charge real funds and test refunds do not return real money. The approved working defaults below are intended for the future live service, pending final legal review and implementation.

Checkout cancellation and pending funding

Leaving Stripe Checkout through its return link opens the canceled page. That page does not charge or credit your account. If you completed payment in another tab or believe a payment went through, check My account and contact support rather than assuming cancellation reversed it. The success page waits for verified payment status; a delay should not require paying a second time.

Payment issues

Report a duplicate, unauthorized or incorrectly credited payment to support with the approximate date, amount and reference if available. Do not send full card details or verification codes. Staff compare the account record with Stripe’s payment/refund status. A refund is not performed merely by clicking a result page or by a balance adjustment in admin.

Unused credit and unopened purchased packs

Approved launch policy: customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of purchase for the unused purchased-credit portion, or cancellation of an unopened purchased pack within 14 calendar days of its purchase. No refund fee will be charged. Approved refunds go to the original payment method. A canceled pack and its credit cannot both be refunded; records must prevent double recovery. Promotional rewards and sellback credit are not converted into refundable cash. Broader mandatory legal rights remain unaffected. This is a planned live-service policy, not an activated refund endpoint.

Opened packs

Approved launch policy: no refund solely because a randomly awarded card is disappointing, duplicated or changes market value. Unauthorized charges, incorrect odds, technical errors and missing awards are investigated separately. Opening animation failures must not require another purchase to recover an already awarded card. Defective goods, wrong items and mandatory consumer remedies remain separately protected. Final legal review is required before this policy governs live purchases.

Sellbacks are different from refunds

Selling an available card back exchanges it for the site-credit amount displayed before confirmation. Confirmation relinquishes the card, marks it sold and makes it unavailable to ship. It is not a bank or card refund and offers no routine cash withdrawal. Verified errors can be reported to support for correction. Sellback credit will not expire under the approved launch policy.

Review holds and reconciliation

Refunds, disputes or mismatched payments can trigger a payment-review hold. Staff verify the provider record and reconcile the affected purchased credit without treating promotional credit as a cash refund. Spent refunded credit may require further review. Account adjustments do not replace a provider refund, and provider processing times are not guaranteed by rippr.

Physical-item concerns and contact

Approved fulfillment policy: ask customers to report wrong or damaged items within 14 calendar days of delivery and provide order details and clear photos when available. This requested reporting period is not an absolute cutoff for mandatory rights. After review, provide an appropriate replacement or refund; rippr pays reasonable return shipping for its errors. Raw-card condition or slab-description mismatches can be reported through the same support route. Physical fulfillment is not active. Contact hello@rippr.gg or Contact support; final return authorization and handling procedures must be implemented before shipping begins.

Refunds & cancellations

Clear distinctions between payments, packs and sellbacks.

Current testing status

This is a review draft. Current Checkout payments, balances and items are sandbox-only. Test payments do not charge real funds and test refunds do not return real money. Production refund eligibility and time limits are not established by this page.

Checkout cancellation and pending funding

Leaving Stripe Checkout through its return link opens the canceled page. That page does not charge or credit your account. If you completed payment in another tab or believe a payment went through, check My account and contact support rather than assuming cancellation reversed it. The success page waits for verified payment status; a delay should not require paying a second time.

Payment issues

Report a duplicate, unauthorized or incorrectly credited payment to support with the approximate date, amount and reference if available. Do not send full card details or verification codes. Staff compare the account record with Stripe’s payment/refund status. A refund is not performed merely by clicking a result page or by a balance adjustment in admin.

Unopened packs and unused credit

A final production policy must specify treatment of unused purchased credit, unopened purchased packs, promotional credit and taxes, including any legally required cancellation rights. No fixed refund window, blanket no-refund rule or automatic cash-redemption right is promised here. Contact support before opening an item whose purchase you dispute.

Opened packs

Random results can include duplicates or items worth less than the opening price. A disappointing outcome does not itself indicate an error. Final cancellation/refund treatment for opened packs requires legal review and clear disclosure before purchase. Defective goods, wrong items, unauthorized charges and mandatory consumer rights require separate consideration; this draft does not exclude those remedies.

Sellbacks are different from refunds

Selling an available card back exchanges it for the site-credit amount displayed at confirmation. It is not a cash refund to a bank account or card. The item becomes sold and is no longer available to ship. Promotional and sellback credit must not be described as withdrawable cash.

Review holds and reconciliation

Refunds, disputes or mismatched payments can trigger a payment-review hold. Staff verify the provider record and reconcile the affected purchased credit without treating promotional credit as a cash refund. Spent refunded credit may require further review. Account adjustments do not replace a provider refund, and provider processing times are not guaranteed by rippr.

Physical-item concerns and contact

For wrong, missing or damaged items, contact support with order details and clear photographs if available. Shipping is currently a test workflow; see the shipping policy for present limitations. Before launch, finalize return authorization, return postage, deadlines, grading/condition disputes and refund methods. Contact hello@rippr.gg or Contact support.

Questions?

Shipping & delivery policy

Current availability

This review draft does not activate physical fulfillment. Shipping requests currently save test records and reserve selected items; no carrier purchase, label, dispatch or shipping charge is triggered. Do not submit a test request expecting delivery.

Selecting items and an address

Choose available, unsold items in My items and review the request and delivery address. Items sold back are not available to ship. A pending shipping request changes their status; if you need to change or cancel a request, contact support before any eventual processing. The current site does not provide a universal self-service cancellation promise.

Quotes, destinations and approval

Approved fulfillment plan: begin with the contiguous United States, tracked shipping and the actual shipping quote approved by the customer before payment. Shipping-provider quotes are not connected. An unavailable or blank estimate does not mean free shipping. Actual charges and applicable taxes must be shown before approval. Carrier, insurance and consolidation details remain subject to operational review. Alaska, Hawaii, US territories and international shipping are not included in the initial plan. Eligible purchase states may be narrower than shipping destinations.

Processing and tracking

Approved target after fulfillment is operational: dispatch within 3–5 business days. This target is not a current dispatch guarantee or an active shipping service. Carrier transit estimates will be displayed separately from preparation time, and customers should be notified of delays. Order tracking currently displays saved test updates and references assigned by staff; a reference alone does not establish carrier acceptance.

Packaging and card condition

Packing illustrations show the intended approach: sleeve, rigid protection and protective outer packaging. They are concepts rather than photographs proving completed shipments. Raw cards are described as Near Mint; slabs carry the listed grading company and grade. Final physical checks, packing procedures, stock verification and insurance arrangements must be operational before fulfillment begins.

Address changes, loss and damage

Review your address before requesting shipment and contact support about corrections before dispatch. For missing, wrong or damaged deliveries, include the request reference and photos when available. Approved policy: rippr handles carrier investigations rather than requiring the customer to pursue the carrier alone; appropriate replacement or refund is determined after review, without disclaiming all responsibility after dispatch. Report wrong or damaged items within 14 calendar days where practical; mandatory rights are not cut off by that requested period. rippr pays reasonable return postage for its errors.

Before physical launch

Physical fulfillment remains disabled until stock controls, quotes and handling procedures are working. The approved targets and remedies above need staffing, carrier and insurance arrangements, a return-authorization process and operational testing before activation. No physical shipment or label is created by this policy page. Contact hello@rippr.gg with questions.

Privacy policy

What rippr stores, shares and uses.

Scope and operator

This review draft covers rippr.gg and its account features. rippr.gg is operated by pullz.gg LLC at the mailing address below. The effective date remains pending approval. Privacy questions can be sent to hello@rippr.gg. It describes the current code, not a completed compliance assessment or a guarantee about every external provider’s practices.

Information you provide

Account information includes your phone number, self-reported date of birth, policy acceptance versions and timestamps, display name, optional profile image, optional email for updates and communication preferences. Shipping requests may include your name, postal address and delivery details. Support records may contain ticket text, replies and optional screenshots. Community features store collection selections and likes. Avoid putting payment details, verification codes or unrelated sensitive information into uploads or support tickets.

Account and transaction records

We store balances and their source buckets, pack openings and awarded items, keep/sell/shipping status, rewards, referrals, request identifiers, payment-review records and audit notes. Stripe Checkout identifiers and verified status are used to reconcile sandbox funding. Authentication and infrastructure providers also process technical/security information such as network and device information as described in their own policies.

Why information is used

Information supports account sign-in, inventory and balance records, requested shipping workflows, rewards, referrals, support, communication preferences, fraud prevention and troubleshooting. Confirmed email addresses receive enabled transactional messages. This draft does not authorize unrelated marketing or a new use of uploaded content.

Providers and payment data

Google Firebase provides authentication, database and file storage; Google reCAPTCHA supports phone sign-in security. Vercel hosts the site. Stripe hosts Checkout and processes payment details; rippr’s own account records do not store full card numbers or CVCs. Resend delivers account emails. Providers may process information outside your state or country under their terms. An eventual shipping provider is not connected; its data handling must be disclosed when selected.

Public versus private information

Display names, profile images and published Community collections can be visible to other visitors. Profile-image URLs may be publicly accessible even when not shown in a collection. Date of birth and acceptance audit records are server-only and are not returned in public profiles or account snapshots. Delivery details, support tickets and payment reviews are restricted in the intended application to the account and authorized staff/server operations. Avoid uploading private material as a profile image. Publishing an image or collection can allow others to save a copy.

Browser storage and sign-in

Firebase uses browser persistence such as IndexedDB, local storage or session storage for sign-in. Remember me changes sign-in persistence. Session storage also holds pending request identifiers and short-lived flow state to recover safely from retries. Security services can use cookies or similar technologies. Clearing site storage may sign you out; it does not itself delete server records. No claim is made here that third-party security services use no tracking technologies.

Email and text choices

Email confirmation is required for account emails. Email updates controls reward/referral notifications; shipping updates is separate. Confirmation, welcome and support messages follow their account-service triggers. Changing your saved email requires confirmation again. Optional text-marketing preferences are stored separately; marketing SMS sending remains off. Phone verification messages are for authentication, not marketing consent.

Retention and deletion requests

Approved operating targets, pending legal review and implementation: account-deletion requests go through support, are acknowledged within five business days and targeted for completion within 30 calendar days, subject to applicable deadlines, identity verification and necessary retention. Resolve pending orders and eligible purchased-credit refunds as part of closure. Retain necessary financial/accounting/refund records for seven years, subject to accountant and legal confirmation; support records for two years after resolution unless needed for an active dispute or legal obligation; security logs for a target of 90 days, longer when needed for an investigation. Remove public profiles promptly after a verified deletion request. Backups, provider records and copies saved by others may persist under their applicable retention processes. A verified, support-assisted sandbox closure workflow now hides public collections, pauses new account activity and requires admin review before staged cleanup. It removes the Firebase sign-in identity and original uploaded profile images, redacts active profile/contact/address and birth-date data, redacts phone numbers from SMS-consent audit events, and deletes transient account-operation payloads and email-verification metadata. Necessary financial, item, order, support and audit records are retained, including pseudonymous identifiers where needed; this is not erasure of every record. Retention targets are not automatic purge schedules: provider/backups handling, legal exceptions and production deletion review remain launch requirements. Signing out or clearing local storage does not delete server records.

Security, children and changes

Access controls, authenticated actions and server-side verification are used, but no system can promise perfect security. Report suspected unauthorized access promptly. Proposed adult eligibility is pending review; the service should not knowingly collect children’s information, and concerns should be reported to support. Before launch, complete a privacy/provider review, applicable rights notices, retention schedule and any required cookie or consent controls. Material policy changes need clear notice and an effective date.

Questions?

Contact support or email hello@rippr.gg.

Spark Points records

Private account records include earned, redeemed and reviewed Spark Points and their transaction history. Authorized staff use these records to resolve payment reviews and prevent duplicate awards. Points are not shown publicly.

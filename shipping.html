Processing & delivery

Submit a request from My items. Your cards move to shipping and your request appears in Order tracking. Processing and delivery estimates are not available yet. Carrier tracking appears once assigned.

Shipping costs

Shipping quotes are not connected yet. Submitting a request does not charge shipping. The final cost requires your confirmation before payment; a pending quote does not mean free shipping.

Protective packaging

The illustrated packing guide below shows the planned process: protect the card, fit the packaging, and secure the outer mailer. The image illustrates the packing process.

Lost or damaged orders

Keep the packaging and take photos of any damage. Contact support with your request number and tracking details so your issue can be reviewed. Carrier coverage and any resolution depend on the shipment details.

Changing your address

Update your saved address for future requests. An address change does not update requests already submitted. Contact support about an existing request before it ships.

Shipping questions?

Where can I see my shipping request?＋

Open Order tracking or select a shipping item in My items. Your account shows the saved request and its latest recorded status.

Can I update my address?＋

Edit your saved address for future requests. For a request already submitted, contact support before making changes to its delivery details.

What happens after I submit?＋

The request is saved to your account and the selected cards move out of your available inventory. Tracking details appear only when they have been assigned.

About this draft

These terms describe rippr’s current sandbox features and proposed service rules. They are a review draft, not final launch terms. Real-money charging and physical fulfillment are not enabled by this document. rippr.gg is operated by pullz.gg LLC at the mailing address below. The effective date and eligible locations remain subject to final approval.

Your account

Sign in using a phone number and verification code. Keep your number and account secure, do not share verification codes, and contact support if you lose access or see an unexpected transaction. You are responsible for information and content you submit. Do not impersonate another person, use another person’s payment method without permission, or attempt to access another account.

Eligibility — proposed rule

Proposed for legal review: accounts and purchases would be limited to adults age 18 or older who may legally use the service where they live. This draft does not establish a launch jurisdiction or implement age verification. No eligibility or regional compliance determination has been made.

Packs and outcomes

Each current pack awards one card. Review its price, contents, rarity/value ranges and displayed odds before opening. Outcomes are random, duplicates are possible, and opening multiple packs does not guarantee a particular rarity or value. The reveal animation does not select the outcome. Displayed values are references, not guaranteed resale prices, investment returns or cash-redemption rights.

Site credit and payments

Current balances, purchases and items are sandbox records with no cash value. Sandbox Stripe Checkout does not charge real money. The intended product uses site credit; sellbacks grant site credit, not cash withdrawals. The current code separates purchased, promotional, sellback and older test credit. Expiration, transferability and production spending/refund rules require final approval; this draft does not create those rules.

Your items and sellbacks

Awarded cards are saved to My items. Keeping a card retains it there. A sellback uses the saved item value shown in the confirmation, changes the item to sold, and grants the displayed site credit. A sold card cannot also be shipped or sold again. Contact support if a sellback appears incorrect. A shipping request reserves the item in the current test workflow; it does not arrange real dispatch.

Rewards and referrals

The Rewards page displays current prizes, chances, eligibility and claim timing. Reward packs appear in inventory and can be opened without another pack-price charge. Referral codes are entered before signup. Eligibility is checked by the service; a visual timer or promotional image is not a guarantee of eligibility. Do not create fake accounts, self-refer or automate claims. Final promotion terms must be reviewed before a real-money launch.

Community and acceptable use

Public collections, display names, profile images and likes may be visible to others. Publish only content you have permission to share. Do not upload private information, abusive or unlawful material, misleading listings or malicious files. Do not exploit bugs, manipulate odds, bypass account restrictions or automate abuse. Report security and content concerns through support.

Errors, reviews and changes

Payment problems may place an account on review hold, pausing openings, sales and shipping requests while records are checked. Contact support about incorrect balances or items. Future material terms or pricing changes need clear notice and an applicable effective date; they should not silently rewrite completed transaction records. Nothing here limits rights or remedies that applicable law makes mandatory.

Related policies and contact

Read the privacy, refund, shipping and pack disclosures linked on this page. Contact hello@rippr.gg or use Contact support. Before launch, counsel must finalize eligibility, governing law, dispute handling, suspension/closure procedures and any liability terms. No arbitration agreement, class-action waiver or blanket release is added by this draft.

Questions?

Contact support or email hello@rippr.gg.

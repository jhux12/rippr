A few things to know.

How many cards are in a pack?

Every pack reveals one card. Open multiple packs to add more cards to your collection.

Where do my cards go?

Your winning card is saved to your account before the reveal. Choose Add to inventory to continue, then find it in My items.

Where can I see the odds?

Each pack page shows rarity odds, item value ranges and grail previews before you open.

How does selling work?

Choose Sell for on the reveal or sell an available card from My items. Confirm the displayed value to add it to your site balance and remove that card from your available inventory.
